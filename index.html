01 The story

My journey

From breathing
to speaking.

My work began with breath. It grew into a belief in what our voices can do.

Every voice has a story. Sometimes, finding it requires courage, resilience and a willingness to step beyond our comfort zone.

After migrating to New Zealand, I experienced the challenge of adapting to a new environment, building a new life and learning to express myself with confidence.

As a Respiratory Scientist, I have always been fascinated by the power of breathing. That passion inspired my 2025 Toastmasters International Speech Contest journey and my message, “Our Power Within.”

That connection between breath, voice and presence shapes how I approach speaking. Together, we can find the message that matters to you and build the confidence to share it.
